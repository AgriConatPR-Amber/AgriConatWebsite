ZZ Plants by AgriConat

The ultimate low-maintenance, resilient indoor plant – elegant, durable, and nearly indestructible.

Advantages of ZZ Plants

  • Extremely low maintenance – thrives with minimal care and forgives neglect
  • Tolerates low to very low light – ideal for offices, apartments, and shaded rooms
  • Drought-resistant thanks to water-storing rhizomes – water every 2–4 weeks
  • Air-purifying qualities – helps remove common indoor toxins like benzene and xylene
  • Attractive, glossy foliage with upright growth – adds elegant greenery to any space
  • Highly resilient to pests and disease – one of the toughest houseplants available

Popular ZZ Plant Varieties

AgriConat offers a selection of classic and standout cultivars of Zamioculcas zamiifolia.

  • Classic ZZ – Deep, glossy green leaves; the timeless standard
  • Raven ZZ – Dramatic dark purple-black mature foliage for a bold look
  • Variegated ZZ – Striking cream, white, or yellow patterns on green leaves
  • Zenzi / Zamicro ZZ – Compact, dwarf form perfect for small spaces and desks

ZZ Plant Care Guide

Follow these simple guidelines to keep your ZZ plant thriving for years.

  • Light: Low to bright indirect light; tolerates very low light but grows slowly
  • Water: Allow soil to dry completely between waterings (every 2–4 weeks); overwatering causes root rot
  • Soil: Well-draining potting mix (cactus/succulent blend or standard mix with added perlite)
  • Temperature & Humidity: 65–80°F (18–27°C); average indoor humidity is fine
  • Fertilizer: Feed lightly once or twice during spring/summer with balanced houseplant fertilizer
  • Toxicity: Mildly toxic to pets and humans if ingested (contains calcium oxalate crystals); keep out of reach of children and animals
